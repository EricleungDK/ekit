# mech-review-mutants/stacks: two stacks' R3 shards merge apart; a result of another stack is one CI entry, never counted; the report fails on it

- story: As a repo owner with two stacks (python in `api/`, rust in `web/`), each with its own R3 shards, I get one comment whose rows keep each stack's R3 apart; when a wiring mistake downloads one stack's shard into the other's merge, that result is one CI `fail` entry naming its stack and folder, is never counted, and fails the check; #42's shard checks still hold per stack.
- covers: #62 62.1 (each `mutants` result carries `stack: {stack, project_dir}` and `shard`; a wrong-stack result gives exactly one CI `fail` entry naming it, both directions); 62.2 (two stacks with their own shards merge into one summary, each stack's R3 kept apart: its row, its R3 timing line, its entries); 62.4 (#42 per stack: duplicate shard index, missing shard, older head, other base, other cap each give their CI entry; a foreign stack's shard with another cap and base is named once as another stack's and never trips the leg's shard checks); 62.5 (the merge's CI entry reaches the sticky comment, in the right stack's row, and fails the check; a clean two-stack report passes)

  4. api's merge given web's shard 1 as its shard 1; web's merge given api's shard 1.
  5. api's merge with shard 0 twice; shard 1 missing; shard 1 edited to cap 5, to the base as head, to another base; and web's shard 1 edited to cap 5 and another base, as api's shard 1.
