# Mech-review envelope

Per check, `<check> <status> <head sha>` copied from it. `skipped`/`n/a`/`partial` = not fully run: a ⚠️ row, with its note as debt. `waived` = ran, and a person let its fail entries through: name who and why (`waiver`), never call it a pass. Quote `summary` only when every check ran in full; never write "all pass" yourself. Envelope `head` ≠ HEAD → stale: rerun mech-review at HEAD first.
